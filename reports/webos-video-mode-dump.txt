==========================================
  KEY FINDINGS - VIDEO RECORDING MODE
==========================================
WM mapping per webOS msm_vfe31.c lines 719-722:
  Preview: WM0 (Y) + WM4 (CbCr)
  Video:   WM1 (Y) + WM5 (CbCr)

1. IDENTICAL to preview mode for most registers!
2. WM0 (Preview Y) ENABLED at 0x04C = 0x01
3. WM1 (Video Y) DISABLED at 0x064 = 0x00
4. WM4 (Preview CbCr) ENABLED at 0x0AC = 0x01
5. WM5 (Video CbCr) DISABLED at 0x0C4 = 0x00
6. PING_PONG = 0x00010000 (WM4 active, not WM0)
7. DEMUX_EVEN/ODD = 0xC9CA (same as preview)
8. XBAR_CFG1 = 0x1A1B (VIDEO_MODE routing)

CONCLUSION: WebOS disables CbCr write masters (WM4, WM5) even
during video recording! Only Y planes captured directly by VFE.
The chroma must be handled differently (software conversion?
or encoding directly from UYVY input?).
