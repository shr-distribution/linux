==========================================
  PHOTO CAPTURE MODE REGISTER DUMP
  Date: 2026-03-31 18:10 CEST
  (Rapid polling during photo capture)
==========================================

=== KEY OBSERVATIONS ===

1. Photo capture uses SAME AXI mode as preview/video:
   - AXI_OUT_MODE = 0x01 when active
   - XBAR_CFG1 = 0x1A1B when active

2. During mode transitions (capture moment):
   - AXI_OUT_MODE briefly goes to 0x00
   - XBAR_CFG1 briefly goes to 0x00
   - Then returns to 0x01 / 0x1A1B

3. CAMIF_STATUS observations:
   - 0x00000000 = normal operation
   - 0x80000000 = bit 31 set during transitions (stop/start indicator)

4. WM0_CFG variations seen:
   - 0x0000012F = normal preview config (width=0x12F=303 words)
   - 0x0000001D = different config seen briefly

5. WM1_WR_CFG (0x070) variations:
   - 0x01C8012F = preview mode (height=0x1C8=456, width=0x12F)
   - 0x002D0240 = seen during capture (different dimensions)
   - 0x00000000 = during reconfiguration

=== SAMPLE CAPTURES DURING PHOTO ===

Stable preview between shots:
  AXI=0x00000001 XBAR=0x00001A1B PP=0x00010011 CAMIF=0x00000000

During capture transition:
  AXI=0x00000000 XBAR=0x00000000 PP=0x00010000 CAMIF=0x80000000
  VFE+0x058: 0x0000001D  (different WM0 config)

Right after capture:
  AXI=0x00000001 XBAR=0x00001A1B PP=0x00010011 CAMIF=0x00000000

==========================================
  CONCLUSIONS FOR MAINLINE DRIVER:
==========================================

1. ALL modes (preview, video, photo) use:
   - AXI_OUT_MODE = 0x01
   - XBAR_CFG1 = 0x1A1B (VIDEO_MODE routing)

2. Our driver change (0x200 -> 0x01) is CORRECT!

3. CAMIF_STATUS bit 31 may indicate stop/reconfigure state

4. PING_PONG toggles normally (0x00010000 <-> 0x00010011)
   when DMA is working correctly

5. Buffer addresses rotate during operation (normal behavior)

==========================================
  VFE AXI/DMA REGISTERS - PHOTO CAPTURE
  Date: 2026-03-31
==========================================

During photo capture, VFE registers show rapid transitions:

=== AXI_OUT_MODE (0x040) ===
- 0x00000001 = Normal streaming
- 0x00000000 = Briefly during reconfiguration

=== CAMIF_STATUS (0x204) ===
- 0x00000000 = Normal (no errors)
- 0x80000000 = Bit 31 set during capture transition

=== WM0 WR_CFG (0x058) Transitions ===
- 0x0000012F = Normal preview (burst=303)
- 0x0000001D = During capture (burst=29)
- 0x00000000 = Cleared during reconfiguration

=== WM4 WR_CFG (0x0B8) Transitions ===
- 0x01300097 = Normal video path (lines=304, burst=151)
- 0x001E000E = During capture (lines=30, burst=14)
- 0x00000000 = Cleared during reconfiguration

=== PING_PONG (0x180) ===
Continues to toggle 0x00010000 <-> 0x00010011
(DMA still working during transitions)

=== KEY FINDINGS ===
1. VFE temporarily reconfigures WMs during photo capture
2. AXI mode briefly goes to 0x00 during transition
3. CAMIF_STATUS bit 31 indicates capture/stop state
4. Write Master burst sizes change significantly
5. DMA continues toggling (no stalls)

This explains why photo capture stays in preview mode -
the VFE just briefly reconfigures for capture then
returns to preview streaming.
